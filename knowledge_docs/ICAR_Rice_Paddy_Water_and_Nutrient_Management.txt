# TITLE: Water-Saving Technologies and Nutrient Management in Lowland and Upland Rice

## SECTION 1: WATER MANAGEMENT AND ALTERNATE WETTING AND DRYING (AWD)
Rice (Oryza sativa) traditionally consumes 3000 to 5000 liters of water per kilogram of grain produced. In Southern India, implementing Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) saves 25% to 30% of irrigation water without reducing yield.
AWD Implementation:
- Field Water Tube: Install a 30 cm perforated PVC pipe (10 cm diameter) in the field with 20 cm underground and 10 cm above surface.
- Water Level Thresholds:
  - From transplanting until 1 week after transplanting: Maintain 2 to 3 cm ponded water to facilitate seedling establishment and suppress weed emergence.
  - Tillering to Panicle Initiation: Allow water level inside the field water tube to drop to 15 cm below soil surface before re-flooding to 3-5 cm depth.
  - Flowering Stage: Soil moisture stress during panicle flowering causes spikelet sterility. Maintain a continuous thin film of water (2 to 4 cm) from flowering to dough stage.
  - Pre-harvest Drainage: Drain field completely 10 to 12 days prior to harvest to promote uniform ripening.

## SECTION 2: NUTRIENT AND FERTILIZER MANAGEMENT
Recommended Fertilizer Schedule for Medium Duration Varieties (120-135 days):
- Total RDF: 120:40:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.
- Split Application:
  - Basal dressing: 25% Nitrogen, 100% Phosphorus, 50% Potassium.
  - Active Tillering (20-25 DAT): 50% Nitrogen.
  - Panicle Initiation (40-45 DAT): 25% Nitrogen, 50% Potassium.
- Zinc Deficiency Correction: Basal application of 25 kg Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4) per hectare prevents Khaira disease and interveinal chlorosis in calcareous soils.

## SECTION 3: PEST AND DISEASE MANAGEMENT
1. Brown Planthopper (Nilaparvata lugens - BPH):
   - Symptoms: "Hopper burn" - drying and wilting of plants in circular patches.
   - Management: Avoid excessive nitrogen application. Drain water from fields for 3-4 days to disturb nymph development. Spray Triflumuron or Pymetrozine 50% WG @ 0.6 g/L.
2. Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae):
   - Symptoms: Spindle-shaped lesions with grey center on leaves; rotten neck.
   - Management: Foliar spray of Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L.
